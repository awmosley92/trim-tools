Notes

Reveals get read to 1/32" here because 1/16" is half of a 1/8" reveal — too coarse to tell you anything. Layout numbers still round to 1/16".

When sources disagree, trust the witness line. A jamb that has moved, a stool set at a new height, or a head casing that was recut all corrupt the arithmetic; the paint line does not.

Leg length assumes the casing lands on the stool. If you're setting the new stool at a different height, the old legs won't fit — the fit check below the sources tells you by how much.

Evidence beats arithmetic. Dry-fit one leg before you commit the whole set.
